# Production Replay Avatar Provenance

The four production Replay avatars are original goDiesel vector animations.

They were authored for this repository from code-defined geometry in `app/scripts/generate-replay-avatars.mjs`.

No third-party illustration, character, image, font, or animation asset is embedded in the files.
