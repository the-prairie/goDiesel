# Night earth

`black-marble-2016.jpg` is NASA Black Marble 2016, the 0.1 degree global color composite (3600 by 1800).

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/

File: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg

NASA Earth Observatory imagery is a work of the United States Government. NASA states that its images are generally not copyrighted. This copy is public domain. It is bundled so the night globe does not fetch or cache map tiles.

The arrival map uses the OpenFreeMap dark vector style (`https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/dark`) live. Those tiles are not baked or cached in this repo, and they are not Google Map Tiles.
