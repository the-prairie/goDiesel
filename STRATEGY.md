# goDiesel Strategy

## Positioning

**Relive where you have been. Discover where to go next.**

goDiesel is a personal, immersive atlas for running and cycling experiences.

## Target Problem

Runners and cyclists can find route data, but they cannot easily tell which routes are worth doing, what a route will feel like, or whether it matches the kind of day they want.

Existing tools expose segments, popularity, and performance data, but offer little curation, atmosphere, memory, or trusted sense of place.

goDiesel begins by turning personal route history into an explorable atlas.

Over time, it can become a Rick Steves-style guide to memorable running and cycling experiences.

## Our Approach

goDiesel turns real route history into an editorial, immersive atlas.

Every route is grounded in actual activity data, then enriched with curation, terrain, replay, and narrative context.

The product helps runners and cyclists relive completed adventures through real-world, video-game-like replays and confidently plan new ones.

## Who It Is For

goDiesel is initially built for Lauren.

Before a trip, she uses it to choose runs and rides that match the experience she wants.

After completing a memorable route, she uses it to preserve, revisit, and share that experience through her personal atlas.

The core loop is:

1. Plan an experience.
2. Complete the route.
3. Curate and preserve it.
4. Relive it through immersive replay.
5. Use that memory to inspire the next adventure.

## Key Metrics

### Planning Usefulness

How often goDiesel helps select a run or ride before a trip.

### Replay Value

How often completed routes are revisited, replayed, or shared.

### Curation Quality

Whether a route page communicates enough about vibe, terrain, difficulty, and significance to make a confident decision.

### Atlas Growth

The number of completed routes preserved with meaningful context rather than stored only as GPS traces.

These are product-learning measures for now, not growth targets.

## Product Tracks

### Immersive Replay

Make completed routes feel explorable and memorable through real-world 3D environments, route-following motion, terrain, avatars, and narrative context.

### Route Curation

Turn raw activity data into useful route guides that explain the vibe, terrain, difficulty, significance, and kind of day each route suits.

### Planning and Finder

Help choose future routes around the world while keeping planned routes distinct from completed memories.

## Product Model

### Atlas

A personal record of completed routes and past experiences.

Atlas answers: **Where have I been, and what was it like?**

### Finder

A planning environment for discovering and evaluating future routes.

Finder answers: **Where should I go next, and which route fits the experience I want?**

## Current Scope

The current product should establish:

- A globe-first personal atlas.
- Completed routes displayed as real route traces.
- Curated route pages with experiential context.
- Immersive replay for completed routes.
- A distinct Finder experience for future route planning.
- Clear navigation between Atlas, Finder, Routes, Replay, and Admin.

## Out of Scope

The following are intentionally deferred:

- Social feeds.
- Leaderboards.
- Travel booking and logistics.
- Native mobile activity tracking.
- Background GPS recording.
- Public route submissions.
- Creator marketplaces.
- Broad community features.

These should not shape the initial architecture or distract from the personal plan, experience, preserve, and replay loop.
